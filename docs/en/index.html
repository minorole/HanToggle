FAQ

Simplified and Traditional Chinese on Mac

What is HanToggle?

HanToggle is a macOS menu-bar utility that converts selected Simplified or Traditional Chinese in place with one global hotkey.

How do I convert Simplified and Traditional Chinese on Mac?

Select the text and press your HanToggle hotkey. The converted text replaces the selection; press again to convert it back.

Does HanToggle upload selected text?

No. Conversion runs on your Mac with no network conversion, analytics, or telemetry, and HanToggle does not log selected or converted text.

Which Macs does HanToggle support?

HanToggle requires macOS 13 or later. Release builds support Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

Is HanToggle free?

Yes. HanToggle is free to download and open source under the MIT License. The DMG and source code are published on GitHub.
